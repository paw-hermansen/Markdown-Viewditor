## Description

Advanced markdown viewer/editor for technical minds - mermaid diagrams, math, chemistry, charts, scroll sync,
CSS themes, many exports

Whether you're writing documentation, notes, technical papers, or blog posts, Markdown Viewditor gives you a
clean, distraction-free editing experience with powerful features built in. Choose from three view modes:
editor-only, split view, or preview-only. The scroll sync keeps your cursor position and preview aligned
automatically.

Show organic molecules from SMILES and draw beautiful charts with inline data. Render mathematical formulas
and chemical equations with built-in KaTeX and mhchem support — including delimiters used by ChatGPT, Claude,
Copilot, and Gemini, so you can paste AI-generated math directly into your documents.

Customize the look with 8 built-in dark and light themes, or create your own with custom CSS. Export your
work to self-contained HTML, ODT (for Word, LibreOffice, and others), or print to PDF. HTML and PDF export
can be fully themed including custom themes.

Markdown compatibility levels let you target basic (CommonMark), GitHub-flavored, or advanced syntax — with
soft editor warnings when your document uses features outside the chosen level.

## Product features

Here they are, trimmed to the essentials:

* **Live preview:** rendered output updates as you type.
* **Three view modes:** editor, split, or preview only.
* **Scroll sync:** the preview follows your cursor.
* **Tabs:** many documents at once, restored after restart.
* **App zoom:** 70% - 300%
* **Math:** equations and chemical formulas (KaTeX, mhchem).
* **Diagrams:** flowcharts, sequence, Gantt and more (Mermaid).
* **Charts:** line, bar, scatter from Vega-Lite JSON.
* **Molecules:** organic chemistry structures from SMILES.
* **Code blocks:** syntax highlighting for dozens of languages.
* **Export:** HTML, ODT, DOCX, PDF and print.
* **Themes:** eight light and dark themes, plus custom CSS.
* **Compatibility levels:** editor warnings for your target Markdown spec.
* **Search and command palette** for keyboard-driven use.
* **YAML frontmatter:** including AI agent SKILL.md files.
* **Native and fast:** built on Rust and Tauri, works offline.
* **Cross platform:** Linux, macOS, Windows.
* **Free and open source:** MIT licensed.
